Privacy Policy.

What data this portfolio collects, why it is needed, and how you can ask for it to be removed.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

1. Who operates this website?

MLB is a personal developer portfolio maintained by Mufaddal Battiwala. You can contact the operator at mufaddal.techpro@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to submit

When you send a contact form, we collect the name, email address, subject, message, and your acknowledgment of this policy. A random receipt ID and submission time are generated. We use these details solely to respond to the inquiry, protect the form from misuse and manage delivery.

3. How messages are handled

Messages are stored in the site's MongoDB database and may be forwarded by authenticated SMTP to the portfolio operator's email inbox. Database records are configured for automatic deletion after 90 days; copies delivered by email can require separate deletion. We do not sell contact data.

4. Abuse prevention and server logs

Form requests are rate-limited using short-lived HMAC-derived client identifiers. Web servers may temporarily process IP addresses and request metadata for security and service operation. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive material through the public form.

5. Site feedback

If you submit site feedback, we store your 1–5 rating, category, optional comment, and submission timestamp for up to 90 days. The feedback form does not ask for a name or email; technical rate-limit identifiers expire independently.

6. Analytics preferences

By default, this website does not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts. The consent banner stores your preference in your browser's local storage; optional analytics will only run if a provider is explicitly configured and you opt in. No analytics provider is preconfigured in this release.

7. Sharing and processors

Database, hosting and email infrastructure providers may process the minimum information necessary to operate the site under their own applicable agreements. External project, GitHub, LinkedIn and article links have separate privacy policies.

8. Your choices and requests

To request access, correction or deletion of a contact inquiry, email mufaddal.techpro@gmail.com with enough detail to identify your message. You can reset the analytics preference by removing this site's local storage in your browser. Applicable legal rights depend on your jurisdiction.

9. Updates

This policy may change when site features or service providers change. Material updates will be reflected on this page along with a revised effective date.
