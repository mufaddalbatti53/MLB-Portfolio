01. Who operates this site?

This personal portfolio is operated by Mufaddal Battiwala (MLB), based in Kolkata, India. It showcases software projects, technical writing and professional experience. For privacy questions or requests, contact mufaddal.techpro@gmail.com.

02. Information we collect

You can browse public pages without creating an account. The following information is processed when you use particular features:

Contact form
Name, email, subject, message, privacy acknowledgment and time, receipt identifier, and message-handling status.
Site feedback
Rating (1–5), category, optional comment and submission time. No name or email is requested, but please avoid including identifying details in comments.
Technical requests
IP addresses and request metadata may be processed by the site and its infrastructure to deliver pages, prevent abuse and diagnose failures. Rate limiting uses keyed, derived client identifiers and temporary counters.
Restricted admin access
Login credentials are processed for authentication. The admin session uses a short-lived token in browser session storage and a corresponding server-side session record.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, medical information or confidential employer or client data through the public forms.

03. Why information is used

  • Respond to inquiries about projects, recruitment and collaboration.
  • Manage the contact inbox and improve the site using feedback.
  • Authenticate the authorized administrator and protect the service from spam or abuse.
  • Deliver the requested pages, maintain the service and investigate technical issues.

Contact information is not sold. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

04. Browser storage and analytics

The current site uses browser storage for essential preferences and restricted administrator access:

mlb-theme
Local storage remembers your Light, Dusk or Dark theme.
mlb-analytics-consent
Local storage remembers your analytics preference. The current release does not send analytics events to a third-party analytics provider, even if you accept.
mlb-admin-token
Session storage holds the authentication token after an authorized admin signs in. Signing out removes it.

You can change the theme using the header switch. To reset the analytics choice, remove the site's stored analytics preference using your browser's site-data settings; the prompt will appear again. Clearing site data may also reset your theme or sign you out. Hosting providers and external websites may use their own storage and logs.

05. Retention and deletion

The application is configured by default to expire contact and feedback records after 90 days. The operator may change these retention settings; database expiry is asynchronous, so removal may not occur at the exact expiry time.

Rate-limit records expire after their configured short windows. Admin session records have their own expiration; the default token lifetime is 15 minutes. If email forwarding is enabled, copies of inquiries may be retained separately in the operator's mailbox. Hosting logs and backups, if present, follow their own retention settings.

Information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, resolving disputes or meeting legal obligations.

06. Hosting and service providers

The intended production architecture uses GitHub Pages to deliver the frontend, Render to run the API and MongoDB Atlas to store application data. These providers may process information needed to deliver their services. Email delivery may also involve an SMTP provider if the operator enables it. Processing may occur in regions outside India, depending on the configured services.

For more information, see the providers' notices: GitHub, Render and MongoDB. Links to social networks and project demos are governed by those sites' own policies.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address a security incident.

07. Security

The application is designed to use HTTPS in production, restricted admin access, password hashing, expiring authentication tokens, request validation and rate limiting. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. If you discover a possible vulnerability, please report it privately using the contact address below.

08. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to submit a form and instead contact the operator directly by email. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted, withdraw permission for optional processing, or raise a privacy concern. Applicable statutory rights depend on your circumstances and the laws in force.

Email mufaddal.techpro@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request” and, if available, your contact receipt ID. Provide only enough information to identify the relevant record. Reasonable verification may be needed before disclosing or deleting information.

09. Children and young visitors

The portfolio is intended for general professional and educational browsing, not as a service directed at children. If you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before submitting personal information. A parent or guardian may contact the operator about information submitted by a child.

10. Changes to this notice

This notice will be reviewed when collection practices, features or providers change. The revision date above identifies the latest published text. If optional analytics is introduced in the future, its details and consent controls should be updated before activation.
